Welcome to Camp Moses

A caring foundation for every learner in Kibale

Camp Moses Junior Academy is a nursery and primary school serving the Kamukalo community in Kibale, Rakai District. We bring together dedicated teachers, a disciplined learning environment, and genuine care for every child who walks through our gate.

Guided by our motto, Education Is The Key To Success, we build strong academic foundations while nurturing confidence, character, and community spirit in every learner — from their first day in Baby Class to the day they graduate in Primary Seven.

Our Foundation

Vision & Mission

What guides Camp Moses Junior Academy every single day.

Our Vision

To raise disciplined, knowledgeable, and God-fearing learners from Kamukalo and beyond who are well prepared to succeed in secondary school and in life.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, quality nursery and primary education in a safe environment — combining strong academics, moral values, and individual attention for every child.

Why Families Choose Us

What makes Camp Moses different

Qualified, Caring Teachers

A committed teaching staff that knows each learner by name and follows their progress closely.

Safe, Welcoming Campus

A secure compound in Kamukalo, Kibale, giving parents peace of mind throughout the school day.

Discipline & Values

Strong moral guidance and structure that shapes respectful, responsible young learners.

Holistic Growth

Academics balanced with sports, music, and co-curricular activities that build well-rounded pupils.

Community Rooted

Proudly serving families in Kibale and the wider Rakai District for generations to come.

Affordable Fees

Quality education at a fair cost, with flexible term-by-term payment support for parents.
